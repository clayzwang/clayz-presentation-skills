# Task-specific meaning

Logic explains the actual subject and relationships in complete prose. Preserve
definitions, scope, evidence strength and uncertainty. Optional semantic metadata
may clarify a complex subject, but no object taxonomy, node tree, parent/child
contract, sibling grammar or fixed method chain is required.

Analytical methods come from the task, personal configuration or selected external
knowledge. They are references for professional judgment, not built-in slide routes.
Copy owns content grouping and copy IDs; Art Direction maps those tags to visual
structure. Do not choose final wording, layout or graphic primitives in Logic.

Keep repeated facts and definitions consistent and make real qualifications clear.
A statement of correlation must not become causation, nor unordered facts a process.
Explain material uncertainty using the existing story and evidence fields.

Use the [current Logic contract](logic-package-contract.md) for complete story
and page allocation. A content unit may be handed to Copy independently when its
meaning and evidence remain understandable without an invented node hierarchy.
