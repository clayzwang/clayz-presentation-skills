# Complete account, editing and text granularity

Save and register the actual audience-facing account before creating pages and text roles. A later concatenation of page text cannot prove this order. Boundaries follow meaning, dependencies, evidence and qualifications, without a target unit count. Text categories are optional; headings require actual explicit levels.

Trace edits to real account spans. Explain consequential transformations and actual relations in existing semantic records. Generic purpose statements are not relation judgments. Text-unit identity does not prescribe boxes, equal weights, columns or visual order. Art independently designs from the whole story. Existing reviewers assess understanding, fidelity and organization; field coverage is not quality.
