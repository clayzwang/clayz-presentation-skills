# Logic learning navigation

Use this area for reusable observations about audience, desired outcome, evidence, definitions, question chains, management stages, causal claims, uncertainty, narrative archetypes, and cross-slide invariants.

- Read shared material through the registry by task purpose and evidence need.
- Append task-grounded observations to `learning-records.jsonl` only after completion.
- Record what was learned, the evidence, the applicable task type, and unresolved limits.
- Do not store final copy, page coordinates, themes, private business data, or generated decks here.
- Do not treat an observation as an approved reference without a human admission record.
