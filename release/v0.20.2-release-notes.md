# Clayz Presentation Skills v0.20.2

Art now connects eleven design concerns to concrete conclusions, actions and a separately downloadable learning pack. The pack includes 66 principle-bearing codes, eight official university teaching sources, 33 paired synthetic teaching examples and an eleven-step hypothetical business example. Principles and examples are original synthesis, not university-authored or human-validated outcomes.

Art learning references are read by code and checked against the selected package when planning is recorded. Conclusions, actions, consulted text and application travel through the locked Art plan and final report. Original Logic/Copy remain intact; Art may revise expression, argument, page order and pagination without advance modification requests. Exact differences with reasons, evidence and impact reach independent final audit. Difference alone does not fail, but unsupported changes cannot receive an unqualified pass.

Download the `art-learning.zip` alongside Cloud Light or Local Light, extract it separately, and follow its README/TXT. Light plugins do not embed the knowledge body. The Windows offline add-on remains available. No eleven-agent expansion, mandatory eleven-stage approvals, layout presets or automatic aesthetic truth were introduced. Historical contracts remain replayable; this release does not claim a measured reduction in production time or improved human-reader results.
