# Clayz Presentation Skills v0.17.4

Logic now delivers research results: supported findings, calculations, source evidence, counterevidence and uncertainty, without preallocating slides. Copy owns the content structure and PPT-suitable text, including chapters, pagination, headings, opening/closing and the wording of presentation conclusions. Art Direction owns the presentation structure: medium, visual hierarchy, tables, charts, logos, numbering and composition. Copy may request these additions; Art records how it implements, adapts or declines them within user requirements.

New runs use package 3.2 and handoff 1.2. Immutable research is bound to finding references and approved data, while the compatibility page projection is explicitly Copy-owned. Art plan 2.0, real image drafts, native Output, independent auditing and the complete formal delivery reports remain in place. Existing 2.4/3.0/3.1 artifacts retain their original semantics and validators. Do not relabel old artifacts; finish an existing run on its original contract or produce a genuine revised handoff.

Validation covers research without pages, Copy repagination, evidence/data tampering, visible required findings, Copy-to-Art requests, legacy replay and the actual CLI report/publication/verify-handoff route. Synthetic tests establish integrity, not the quality of research, Chinese prose or native PowerPoint/WPS rendering. No private documents, fonts or masters are bundled.
