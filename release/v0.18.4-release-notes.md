# Clayz Presentation Skills v0.18.4

Art judges page balance from the combined visible weight of color, filled area,
position, grouping and text density. A large saturated supporting panel can
overpower the main evidence even when every box is aligned and all text fits.
Art identifies the actual attention problem, revises its cause and reinspects
the color draft at thumbnail and full size, together with neighboring pages.

The bilingual core guidance preserves purposeful asymmetry and gives practical
ways to reconsider color coverage, intensity, emphasis and space. Observations
remain in existing Art work notes. There is no color-area quota, symmetry rule,
automatic balance score or new audit form.

Copy wording, paragraph and heading rules, Copy 3.3, Art 2.2, native editing
boundaries and audit contracts retain their existing behavior. This instruction
update does not itself establish that any revised presentation is balanced.
